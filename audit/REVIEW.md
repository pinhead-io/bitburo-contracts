# Internal reviews of the Rebalancer

## 1. Independent review, 17 September 2026

A reviewer that had not written the code read `src/Rebalancer.sol`, the interfaces, the deploy script, the tests and the mocks, ran the suites, wrote PoCs and ran 28 behavioural mutants against the test suite, all outside the repository. Result: **no way to move user funds outside an intended run, and nothing above Low.** Every finding and what was done with it:

| # | Severity | Finding | What was done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low (functional) | With the band computed from rounded values, a wallet exactly on the band edge was decided by rounding dust: all cbBTC with a 95% target was due for 19 of 20 totals at a non-round price, and sold 5% of the wallet. | **Fixed.** The comparison is exact (`position × BPS` against `total × targetBps`, `drift <= total × BAND_BPS`). Test: `test_execute_bandEdgeDoesNotDependOnRounding`. A target within 5% of 0% or 100% only ever trades in one direction; README says so. |
| 2 | Low (functional) | A correction larger than the 10% cap stopped inside the band, up to 5 points short of the target (all cash with a 74% target ended at 69.9%). | **Fixed.** A run that leaves at least 20 USDC of its correction undone marks the mandate `unfinished`; the next day's run continues to the target, also inside the band. The flag expires after two days without a run and is cleared by `setMandate` and `stop`. Tests: `test_execute_correctionLargerThanTheCapIsFinishedOnTheNextDays`, `test_execute_unfinishedCorrectionExpiresAfterTwoIntervals`, `test_setMandate_startsFromTheBandAgain`. |
| 3 | Low (test quality) | The reentrancy test could not fail: it re-entered from `balanceOf` (a static call) and from an address that was not the keeper. | **Fixed.** The token is now the victim's keeper and re-enters from `transfer`; the test expects `ReentrancyGuardReentrantCall` and shows that the same call outside `sweep` is an ordinary run. |
| 4 | Low (test gap) | No deterministic test pinned "`stop` then `setMandate` does not reset the interval" and "`retire` is irreversible". | **Fixed.** `test_stop_thenSetMandateDoesNotShortenTheInterval`, and `test_retire_onlyTheTreasury` calls `retire` twice. |
| 5 | Info | Two rounded-down divisions loosened the lower bound at extreme prices; at 1e15 USDC per cbBTC a 20 USDC buy that returned 0 sats passed. | **Fixed.** The lower bound rounds up and a swap that delivers nothing is refused. Tests: `test_execute_lowerBound`, `test_execute_zeroOutputIsRefused`. |
| 6 | Info | Tests that could not fail or hid behaviour: the 100k fork test passed in both branches; the fee invariant derived the fee from the balance it then asserted on; the mock router never filled partially; nothing donated tokens to the contract; fuzz tests only used a round price. | **Fixed.** The 100k fork test asserts success (and skips itself when the pool stands more than 0.20% from the feed); the handler takes the fee from the `Executed` event and donates tokens to the contract, and the invariant checks that those end up with the treasury and never in a run; the mock can fill partially (`test_execute_partialFillIsRefused`); the fuzz tests draw the price. |
| 7 | Info | Wording: `retire()` "once" (it can be called again, harmlessly); `OutsidePriceBounds` says "less" while the real router reports too little as `Too little received`; "at most 10% of the wallet" does not hold below 200 USDC, where the 20 USDC minimum is the budget. | **Fixed** in NatSpec and README; `test_execute_tinyWalletMayTradeMostOfItself` pins the small-wallet behaviour. |
| 8 | Info | `MAX_FEED_AGE` 1500 s leaves 268 s above the largest heartbeat gap seen in 59 hours (1232 s). | Accepted: availability only; a skipped run is retried by the keeper's next run. |
| 9 | Info (keeper) | `minAmountOut` is an absolute amount from a simulation: it does not protect against a pool pushed before the simulation, and turns loose if the run grows before inclusion. | Accepted: the contract's own bound still applies; a third party would have to donate to the owner to gain a fraction of the donation. |
| 10 | Info (keeper) | Anyone can emit `MandateSet(x, t, thisKeeper)` for ~46k gas, so the keeper's owner list can be spammed. | **Fixed in the keeper and corrected here:** owners whose mandate does not name the keeper are forgotten after the read (`withoutOwners`); owners that do name it cost two view calls per run in a Multicall3. There is no on-chain registry any more, and the app serves its own users from its own records. |
| 11 | Info | solc 0.8.37 was one week old on the review date; its known-bugs list is empty. | Noted. Every earlier release back to 0.8.28 has three to seven known bugs (none reachable from this contract's code); the choice of 0.8.37 predates this rewrite (PR #15). A question for the external audit. |

Checked and fine, among others: only the owner or the appointed keeper can run a wallet and output only goes to the owner (mutants killed); `amountIn == 0` and the router's special recipients are unreachable; a partial fill far below the bound cannot pass it (one that meets the bound is refused since 18 September, finding 13); leftover router allowance is harmless because the router only pulls from its caller; fees held by the contract do not leak into a sell; `isDue` and `execute` agree and a buy never exceeds the cash balance (20,000 fuzz runs at random prices); the direction never flips the next day at any pool deviation inside the bound (a price move or the owner's own transfer can make a follow-up trade back, finding 14); every wrong constructor address ends in a dead deployment, never in a user loss; transient storage works on Base.

Not verified by the reviewer: byte equality of the Base router and pool with the published sources, how Chainlink builds cbBTC/USD and behaves under congestion, a real partial-fill PoC on a fork (covered by reasoning and by the mock).

## 2. Tools, fuzzing and the oracle, 18 September 2026

Slither and Aderyn with every result checked against its call path, every entry point and the price reviewed by hand, and the invariants of `SCOPE.md` §3 run as 20 properties in a Medusa and Echidna harness (`fuzz/`). Result: nothing above Low; one finding changed the contract.

| # | Severity | Finding | What was done |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | Low | Chainlink lists the Base cbBTC/USD feed with the methodology `dex_state_price` (end-of-block states of onchain DEX pools, weighted by volume, with outlier and time-lag filters), so the price bound is not fully independent of the pools on Base. A push held across blocks and across those pools can move the answer, and a due run then trades at the moved answer, within the bound. Not reproducible: Chainlink does not publish the pools or their weights. | **Accepted and documented** (`SCOPE.md` §4 risk 10 and question 6; README). Measured alternative: Chainlink BTC/USD (CEX prices, 0.1% deviation). Sampled every 6 hours over 30 days, the pool stood at most 15.3 bps from it against 30.6 bps from cbBTC/USD (`feed-vs-pool.py`). The contract keeps cbBTC/USD. |
| 13 | Info | A pool that runs dry takes only part of the input, and SwapRouter02's `exactInputSingle` does not check that it took everything. When what arrived still met the bound on the whole input, the run went through and the rest (up to ~1.1% of the run) stayed in the contract with an allowance to the router, for `sweep` to send to the treasury. The owner's loss stayed within 0.80%. On Base it was not reachable: at block 51,464,439 the pool took swaps of 1 billion USDC and of 100,000 cbBTC in full (`fuzz/PoolDepthFork.t.sol`). | **Fixed.** `_swap` also refuses a run after which the router still has an allowance (`:326-329`), with the existing `OutsidePriceBounds`, so the ABI and the keepers stay as they are. Tests: `test_execute_partialFillWithinTheBoundIsRefused` (fails without the check) and `test_fork_tokensLowerTheAllowanceOnTransferFrom` (the check relies on USDC and cbBTC lowering an allowance by what `transferFrom` moves). Cost: about 1,800 gas per run on the fork. |
| 14 | Info | A follow-up run ignores the band and goes to the target at that day's price, so after a price move or the owner's own transfer it can trade back, inside the band: 43 USDC on a 10,000 USDC wallet 0.43% from its target. Nobody can cause it for profit; the owner pays fee and slippage on that trade. | **Accepted and documented** (README, `SCOPE.md` §4 risk 11). Test: `test_execute_followUpGoesToTheTargetFromEitherSide`. |

**Tools.** Slither and Aderyn: every result is by design or a false positive, each checked against its call path. The Slither MCP server was not connected, so the call paths come from the Slither printers.

**Properties.** `fuzz/RebalancerHarness.sol` deploys the contract against the unit-test mocks and only makes the calls real accounts can make: three owners set, change and stop mandates, change allowances, receive and spend tokens; the owner, its keeper, another owner, a stranger or the treasury tries `execute` with no, a tight or an impossible `minAmountOut`; anyone donates to the contract or sweeps it; the treasury retires it, rarely; the price moves up to 20% at a time and the pool stands up to 75 bps from the feed either way; time passes by up to three days. The 20 properties: only the owner or its keeper runs a wallet; at most one run per day; no run after `retire` or without a mandate; `isDue` announces exactly the run; a run is at least 20 USDC and at most max(10%, 20 USDC); the loss stays within 80 bps and the swap output within ±55 bps of Chainlink; a run goes towards the target and not past it; no run inside the band except a follow-up, and the follow-up flag follows the rule; the output goes to the owner and meets `minAmountOut`; a run leaves only the fee in the contract, fees and donations leave only through `sweep` to the treasury, and no allowance to the router survives; other wallets, their allowances and mandates stay untouched; a run `isDue` announced goes through when the pool is sound; `setMandate`, `stop` and `retire` behave as documented.

| Run | Result |
|---|---|
| Medusa, 600 s, 10 workers | 4,315,304 calls; all 20 hold. 87 of 95 lines of `Rebalancer.sol` reached; not reached: the getters of the immutables and of `MAX_DEVIATION_BPS`, and `revert SequencerDown()`, which the unit tests cover. |
| Echidna, 10 workers | 500,405 calls; all 20 hold; the same lines reached. |
| Canaries (`RebalancerCanaries`) | the fuzzer reached a buy, a sell, a follow-up inside the band, a follow-up that trades back (finding 14) and `retire`. |
| A pool that runs dry (`RebalancerPartialFill`) | before the fix, three properties failed within two calls (finding 13); with the fix, all 20 hold for 1,672,567 calls. |
| With the fix, Medusa, 300 s | 2,386,619 calls; all 20 hold. |
| Nine planted bugs, 60 s each | all nine caught: keeper check removed; interval not written; upper bound removed; follow-up never expires; bought cbBTC sent to the caller; band halved; `setMandate` keeps the follow-up flag; sell fee not deducted; cap doubled. |

**Checked by hand.** Every division rounds in the owner's favour or by less than one sat, and the interval rules out repeating a run to collect the rounding. Only `sweep` calls an address the caller chooses, and it shares the reentrancy guard with `execute`. The mandate is written before the first token transfer, and a revert undoes it. SwapRouter02 pulls only from its caller, during that caller's own swap (`payer: msg.sender` in `V3SwapRouter.sol`), so no one else could have spent an allowance left behind. The sequencer check follows Chainlink's own sample and is stricter in two cases (`startedAt` 0 or in the future). The cbBTC/USD aggregator has no floor or ceiling to stick at (`minAnswer` 1, `maxAnswer` ≈ 9.6e52). solc 0.8.37 still has no known bugs and there is no newer release; no advisory covers OpenZeppelin 5.6.1.

**Earlier decisions, re-examined.** The single cbBTC/USD feed: see finding 12. The follow-up rule: kept; dropping it would save about eight lines but leave corrections larger than the cap up to five points short of the target. Trusting the balance difference over the router's return value: kept, and it is what made finding 13 visible. `retire` in the treasury's hands, `isDue` next to `execute`, USDC counted as one dollar and solc 0.8.37: kept.

Not verified: Chainlink's off-chain aggregation for cbBTC/USD; a real partial fill on Base (the pool cannot be drained on a fork without rewriting its storage); the keeper script; formal verification.
